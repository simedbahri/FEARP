## 🚀 Application Process

### Step 1: Prepare Your Site
1. **Add Quality Content**
   ```
   - Create 10-15 articles through admin panel
   - Each article: 300+ words, original content
   - Include relevant images
   - Proofread for errors
   ```

2. **Verify All Pages Work**
   - Test all links in footer
   - Check privacy policy is accessible
   - Verify contact page works
   - Test on mobile devices

3. **Remove Any Prohibited Content**
   - No adult content
   - No copyrighted material
   - No misleading information
   - No clickbait headlines

### Step 2: Apply for AdSense
1. **Go to Google AdSense**
   - Visit: https://www.google.com/adsense/
   - Sign in with your Google account
   - Click "Get Started"

2. **Enter Your Website**
   - Enter your website URL (e.g., https://fearp.vercel.app)
   - Select your country
   - Choose payment method

3. **Add Your Site**
   - Enter your site URL
   - Select your site type (Blog/Website)
   - Answer questions about your content

4. **Submit for Review**
   - Review all information
   - Submit application
   - Wait for review (usually 1-2 weeks)

### Step 3: While Waiting for Approval
1. **Continue Adding Content**
   - Keep publishing new articles
   - Show active site
   - Improve existing content

2. **Monitor Your Site**
   - Check for broken links
   - Fix any errors
   - Improve page speed

3. **Build Traffic** (Optional but helpful)
   - Share on social media
   - Post on forums
   - Engage with your audience

## ⚠️ Common Rejection Reasons

### Content Issues
- ❌ **Insufficient content** - Less than 10 articles
- ❌ **Low-quality content** - Poor grammar, thin content
- ❌ **Copied content** - Duplicate from other sites
- ❌ **Prohibited content** - Adult, violence, etc.

### Technical Issues
- ❌ **Site not accessible** - 404 errors, broken links
- ❌ **No privacy policy** - Required by law
- ❌ **Poor navigation** - Hard to find pages
- ❌ **Not mobile-friendly** - Must work on phones

### Policy Violations
- ❌ **Clickbait** - Misleading headlines
- ❌ **Copyright violations** - Using others' content
- ❌ **Fake traffic** - Bot traffic, click farms
- ❌ **Under construction** - Site not ready

## 🎉 After Approval

Once approved:
1. You'll receive email notification
2. Add your Publisher ID to `AdConfig.ts`
3. Create ad units in AdSense dashboard
4. Update ad slot IDs in `AdConfig.ts`
5. Update `ads.txt` file
6. Ads will start showing automatically!

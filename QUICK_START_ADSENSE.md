**Goal**: Create 10-15 original articles

1. Go to your admin panel: `https://your-site.com/admin212`
2. Log in with your Firebase credentials
3. Click "Add New Article"
4. Write original content (300+ words minimum)
5. Add images if possible
6. Save and publish

**Article Ideas:**
- "10 Creative DIY Projects for Beginners"
- "Latest Fashion Trends for 2025"
- "How to Start a Creative Blog: Complete Guide"
- "5 Beauty Tips Everyone Should Know"
- "Step-by-Step Tutorial: [Your Topic]"
- "Product Review: [Product Name]"
- "7 Ways to Stay Inspired Daily"
- "The Ultimate Guide to [Your Niche]"
- "Top 10 [Your Topic] Tips"
- "How to [Solve a Problem]"

**Content Tips:**
- Write in your own voice
- Provide real value to readers
- Use headings (H2, H3) to organize
- Include lists and bullet points
- Add images with descriptive alt text
- Make it scannable and easy to read

### Step 3: Apply for AdSense

**When to Apply:**
- ✅ You have 10+ quality articles
- ✅ All content is original
- ✅ Site is live and working
- ✅ All pages accessible

**How to Apply:**
1. Go to: https://www.google.com/adsense/
2. Sign in with Google account
3. Click "Get Started"
4. Enter your website URL
5. Complete the application
6. Submit and wait (1-2 weeks)

## 💡 Content Quality Tips

1. **Be Original**
   - Write in your own words
   - Share your own experiences
   - Don't copy from other sites

2. **Provide Value**
   - Answer reader questions
   - Solve problems
   - Share useful information

3. **Be Engaging**
   - Use conversational tone
   - Include examples
   - Add personal touches

4. **Be Professional**
   - Check grammar
   - Proofread carefully
   - Format properly

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

- ❌ Don't copy content from other sites
- ❌ Don't use AI-generated content without editing
- ❌ Don't publish thin content (< 300 words)
- ❌ Don't use clickbait headlines
- ❌ Don't publish incomplete articles
- ❌ Don't ignore grammar and spelling

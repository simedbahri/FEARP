## 📋 Step-by-Step: Get Your Site Indexed

### Step 1: Set Up Google Search Console

**Why it's important:**
- Submit your sitemap to Google
- See which pages are indexed
- Monitor search performance
- Get notified of issues

**How to set it up:**

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Sign in with your Google account

2. **Add Your Property**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Enter your website URL: `https://fearp.com`
   - Choose verification method (see below)

3. **Verify Ownership**

   **Option A: HTML Tag (Recommended)**
   - Google will give you a meta tag like:
     ```html
     <meta name="google-site-verification" content="YOUR_CODE_HERE" />
     ```
   - I'll help you add this to your site

   **Option B: HTML File Upload**
   - Download the HTML file Google provides
   - Upload it to your site's root directory

   **Option C: Domain Name Provider**
   - Add a TXT record to your DNS

4. **After Verification**
   - You'll see your site dashboard
   - This may take a few minutes to a few hours

### Step 2: Submit Your Sitemap

Once verified:

1. **In Search Console, go to Sitemaps**
   - Left sidebar → Sitemaps

2. **Submit Your Sitemap**
   - Enter: `https://fearp.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

3. **Wait for Processing**
   - Google will crawl your sitemap
   - This can take a few days

### Step 3: Request Indexing for Important Pages

1. **Use URL Inspection Tool**
   - In Search Console, use the search bar at top
   - Enter your homepage: `https://fearp.com`
   - Click "Request Indexing"

2. **Request Indexing for Key Pages**
   - Homepage: `https://fearp.com`
   - About: `https://fearp.com/about`
   - Contact: `https://fearp.com/contact`
   - Privacy Policy: `https://fearp.com/privacy-policy`

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Fixes

### Issue: Pages Not Indexing

**Possible Causes:**
- Noindex tag (check your pages)
- Blocked by robots.txt (check `/public/robots.txt`)
- Low-quality content
- Duplicate content

**Fixes:**
- Remove noindex tags
- Check robots.txt allows crawling
- Improve content quality
- Make content unique

### Issue: Slow Indexing

**Possible Causes:**
- New site (normal for first few weeks)
- No backlinks
- Low traffic
- Technical issues

**Fixes:**
- Be patient (new sites take time)
- Get backlinks
- Share content
- Fix technical issues
